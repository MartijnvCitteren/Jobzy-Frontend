Snappy and minimal. One shared easing across the system: 280ms ease-out. Never stack more than one motion moment on a screen at once — no shimmer, pulse and pop-in together.

- **Hover feedback**: background/border tint only, 200ms — never a shadow or a lift.
- **Loading**: animated bars matching the final content's shape — never a spinner.
- **Advice arrival**: one ring pulse, once, on arrival — never looping.
- **Candidate hired**: a single check pop-in — celebratory but still "calm advisor," never confetti.
